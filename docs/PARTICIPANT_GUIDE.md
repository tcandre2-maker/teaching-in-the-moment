# Participant Guide
## Teaching in the Moment: Using Incidental Teaching to Expand Learning Opportunities in ABA Sessions

Welcome to **Teaching in the Moment**. This self-paced learning module is designed for Registered Behavior Technicians who want to become more effective at recognizing naturally occurring learning opportunities while following the client's written treatment plan.

## What you will learn

You will practice how to:

1. Recognize natural teaching opportunities and distinguish them from nonexamples.
2. Connect an activity or learner response to an authorized treatment-plan goal.
3. Follow the plan's directions for waiting, prompting, reinforcement, and recording.
4. Recognize when to pause and consult the supervising BCBA.

## Before you begin

- Use a current browser on a computer or tablet.
- Turn on audio or connect headphones if you want to listen to narration.
- You can read the transcript when audio is unavailable or when you prefer text.
- Complete the diagnostic pre-assessment before opening the instructional sections.
- Work through the scenarios thoughtfully. They are fictional practice cases, not directions for a specific client.

## How to use the activities

### Flip cards
Select or activate a card to turn it over and reveal the additional information. Activate it again to return to the front.

### Click-to-reveal information
Select the labeled term or control to reveal its explanation. Some notes provide additional context and are not themselves the definition of a nearby term.

### Classification matching
Drag an example into its matching category. If drag-and-drop is difficult or unavailable, select an example first and then select the category. The activity is designed to support mouse, touch, and keyboard interaction.

### Branching scenarios
Read the scenario and choose the response you believe is most consistent with the written plan. Your selection determines the next scenario state and the feedback you receive. Read the consequence and clinical explanation before continuing. You may restart a practice scenario when that option is provided.

### Plan interpretation
Select each treatment-plan annotation to review what that plan component tells you to verify before acting.

### Audio and transcripts
Use the play control to start narration. Use the pause/resume control when available. Open the transcript control to read the narration. Audio is optional; the text content remains the primary reference.

## Your decision-making framework

Use the six-step sequence when the written plan already defines the relevant goal and procedure:

1. **Observe** what the learner is doing.
2. **Recognize or use an authorized opportunity** that naturally occurs in the activity.
3. **Allow the response opportunity** specified in the written plan.
4. **Prompt only if needed** and only as described in the plan.
5. **Provide the programmed reinforcement or outcome** as written.
6. **Record the response** according to the plan.

There is no universal wait time or universal prompt hierarchy. The client's written plan determines the procedure.

## Clinical boundaries

As an RBT, implement the treatment plan under supervision. Do not independently:
- create a new treatment target;
- change the response definition or mastery criterion;
- choose a different prompt hierarchy;
- change reinforcement procedures;
- remove access to communication to force another response form;
- create unnecessary barriers or contrived opportunities not authorized by the plan.

If a clinically meaningful step is missing or unclear, pause the uncertain procedure and consult the supervising BCBA. Follow organizational policy and applicable safety procedures.

## Assessment

The program begins with a 20-item diagnostic pre-assessment. It is intended to establish a baseline and does not determine whether you pass the final assessment.

At the end, complete the 20-item scenario-based final assessment. The stated mastery requirement is:
- at least 16 of 20 correct; and
- all five designated scope/consultation items correct.

A passing digital score reflects performance on the module assessment. It does not replace direct observation, rehearsal, supervision, or a live competency assessment.

## If something does not work

- If audio does not play, read the transcript.
- If dragging is difficult, use click-to-select matching.
- If animation is reduced or disabled by your device settings, use the visible controls and text.
- If progress is lost, browser storage may have been cleared or private browsing may be in use. Contact your facilitator if you need help.
- If a button or activity does not respond, note the section, activity, browser, and device and tell your facilitator.

## Notes and reflection

Use these prompts after completing the module:
- What observable learner behaviors might signal a natural opportunity?
- How will you check whether the relevant goal and setting are covered by the plan?
- What will help you preserve independent responding?
- What information would prompt you to stop and consult the BCBA?

**Remember:** Teaching in the moment does not mean improvising treatment. It means recognizing an authorized opportunity and implementing the written plan with attention to learner motivation, independence, dignity, and safety.
